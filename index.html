WHO WE ARE

About Our Chapter

The National Association of Polytechnic Students (NAPS), Enugu State Chapter, represents and supports the interests of polytechnic students across Enugu State. Our chapter promotes student leadership, unity, collaboration, educational development and engagement on issues affecting polytechnic students. Visit the official NAPS Enugu State Chapter website for chapter news, announcements, events, programs and leadership updates.

Unity

Building stronger relationships among students and leaders.

Leadership

Encouraging responsible leadership and representation.

Development

Supporting student empowerment and educational opportunities.
